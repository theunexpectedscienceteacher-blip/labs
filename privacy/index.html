Privacy & Student Data

The short version: this site does not collect, store or share any student information. There are no accounts, no logins, no tracking and no ads.

What this site does and does not do

QuestionAnswer
Do students create accounts or log in?No. There are no accounts of any kind.
Does the site collect names, emails, grades or other personal information?No. Nothing a student types is ever sent to a server.
Where is student work saved?Only in the browser on the student's own device (browser local storage), so they can come back to a lab. It never leaves that device.
How do students turn in work?Each lab builds a summary that the student copies and pastes into their teacher's learning management system (for example Schoology, Canvas or Google Classroom), or prints.
Cookies, analytics, trackers or ads?None. No cookies, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party scripts.
Does the site load content from other websites?No. Every page, image and script comes from this site.
Can students reach other people's content or chat?No. There is no user-generated content, commenting, chat or file uploading.

Technical safeguards

  • HTTPS only: every page is served over an encrypted connection.
  • Content Security Policy: each page tells the browser to block all outgoing network requests (connect-src 'none') and to load nothing from other websites.
  • No referrer data: links do not pass page information to other sites.
  • Static site: there is no database or server-side code that could store student data.

Shared devices

Saved work stays in that device's browser until it is cleared. At the bottom of every lab there is a Clear my work from this device button. Students on shared Chromebooks should press it when they finish.

Clearing the browser's site data or using a guest/incognito window also removes all saved work.

For district IT approval

This site is a single, static educational website owned and maintained by The Unexpected Science Teacher. All labs are served from labs.theunexpectedscienceteacher.com. Because the site collects nothing, no student data is shared with The Unexpected Science Teacher or with anyone else. If your district needs anything else to review the site, please use the contact form on theunexpectedscienceteacher.com.
