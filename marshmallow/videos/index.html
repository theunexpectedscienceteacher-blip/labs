Marshmallow Meltdown

Video Lab: Watch & Record

Real marshmallows, heated three ways in a real classroom. Watch each video at least twice, then fill in your sheet. The videos have no sound.

AConduction

Heat moves by touching. The marshmallow rests on a foil-covered hot plate.

Watch for: What happens to the side touching the foil?

BConvection

Heat rides along with moving air or water. The marshmallow is held in the steam rising from boiling water.

Watch for: Does it change shape, or just the outside?

CRadiation

Heat travels as invisible waves. The marshmallow is held under a heat lamp. Nothing touches it.

Watch for: What happens to its size?
